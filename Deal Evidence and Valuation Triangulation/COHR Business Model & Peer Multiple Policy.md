products in this segment. According to COHR 10-K from 2026, FY2026 records revenues of $5.275 billion which is
around 74% of Coherent's total revenue with the majority of the demand being driving be AI datacenters. The 
other segment is the industrial side of Coherent which includes making lasers, components, and materials used 
in manufacturing, semiconductor equipment, and other scientific applications. This accounts for $1.844 billion

over $700 million in 2026 due to the increased demand for its products. While rapid sales growth raises 
accounting profit, their aggressive expansion consumes cash quickly.

**Peer Comparison Policy: Business Economics and Exclusions (My Judgements, No AI Generation)**

In order to justify the comparisons between companies with similar businesses, I must first lay down a policy
that explains which business economies must match and which differences qualify or should exclude certain 
companies. One of the biggest factors in this comparison is scale: COHR is a global corporation that has over 
130 locations worldwide from the United States to northeastern Asia to Europe to South Africa. Companies lack-
ing similar scale will be more difficult to compare; therefore, comparisons will be limited to companies that
operate globally (more than one continent). In addition, about three quarters of COHR's business is concentrat-
ed on datacenters and communications. I will limit comparisons to companies who generate at least 50% of their
revenue from datacenter and/or communication related business segments. The last major exclusion will limit 
comparisons to companies that face similar customer demand or production capacity. Companies with a market cap 
below $5 billion will be excluded from the peer comparison. 

On the otherhand, some items that will qualify a company are a similar product mix (i.e. optical components, 
transceivers, or lasers used in datacenters or communication networks), similar costs and investment needs 
(i.e. large investment in R&D, manufacturing equipment, and inventory), and similar demand factors (i.e. 
benefits from AI expansion, datacenter construction and infrastructure, faster networks, etc.). Companies must
have have some, but do not require all, items that qualify them to be compared, but will automatically be 
excluded if one of the exclusion criterion is met.
