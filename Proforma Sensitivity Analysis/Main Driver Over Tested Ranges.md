# Main Driver Over Tested Ranges

For Coherent, **gross margin is the larger driver of FY2031 signed FCFE including net borrowing among the valid tested cases**. Its FCFE span is **$665.298 million**, compared with **$406.768 million** for revenue growth. Higher gross margins increase after-tax earnings, reduce inventory funding under the model’s COGS-based inventory rule, and reduce the need for earlier revolver borrowing and its FY2031 repayment. More efficient internal manufacturing provides a business rationale for the margin assumption.

**Revenue growth is the larger driver of FY2031 operating profit (EBIT)**: its valid-case span is **$615.429 million**, versus **$233.172 million** for gross margin. Customer demand for Coherent’s AI data-center optical products supports sales growth, but additional sales also require inventory and capital spending, so higher profit does not automatically produce higher final-year FCFE.

These comparisons use the locked FY2027–FY2031 shifts of **±5 percentage points for revenue growth** and **±2 percentage points for gross margin**, changing one input at a time. Spans are maximum minus minimum across valid runs only: lower/base growth and base/higher gross margin. Higher growth and lower gross margin fail funding checks and are excluded, not ranked. Because the input ranges differ and opposite endpoints are invalid, this is a range-specific comparison, not a universal ranking of the drivers. **Value per share remains unavailable** because the original positive-only FCFE valuation and terminal treatment are unresolved.
